What poor maintenance can cost you

Skipped maintenance rarely stays a small problem. These are the risks that build up when an STP is left alone.

System breakdowns

Unexpected failures can disrupt operations and cause costly shutdowns.

Higher repair costs

Small issues today can turn into major problems and expensive repairs.

Permit violations

Non-compliance with DENR and LLDA regulations may result in fines and penalties.

Environmental harm

Poorly maintained STPs can cause pollution and harm aquatic life and surrounding areas.

Damaged reputation

Environmental incidents can affect your brand image and stakeholder trust.

Financial losses

Downtime, fines and repairs add up, hurting your bottom line.

The benefits of regular maintenance

  • Consistent and efficient operationKeeps your STP performing at its best every day.
  • Longer equipment lifeProper care reduces wear and extends the life of your system.
  • Cost savingsPreventive maintenance is always more affordable than major repairs.
  • Regulatory complianceStay compliant with DENR and LLDA requirements with peace of mind.
  • Environmental protectionHelps safeguard our water resources for present and future generations.
