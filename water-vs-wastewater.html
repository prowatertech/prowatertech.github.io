Water treatment vs wastewater treatment

Different sources. Different processes. One goal.

Clean water. Better life. Sustainable future. Here is how the two kinds of treatment differ, and what happens at each stage.

Talk to an expert See the process

Water treatment

Makes raw water safe and suitable for drinking, cooking and other domestic or industrial uses.

Purpose
To remove impurities, chemicals and pathogens from raw water to produce clean, safe, high-quality water.
Sources
Rivers, lakes, groundwater and other natural sources.
End result
Clean, safe water for consumption and use.

Wastewater treatment

Removes contaminants from used water before it is safely returned to the environment or reused.

Purpose
To remove pollutants, organic matter and harmful substances from wastewater, protecting the environment and supporting sustainability.
Sources
Domestic sewage, industrial effluent, commercial wastewater and runoff.
End result
Treated water that is safe to release back to nature or reuse for other purposes.

Sludge line: settled sludge goes to a sludge collection tank, then a filter press. Solids go to compost or disposal, and the liquid returns to the equalization tank.
